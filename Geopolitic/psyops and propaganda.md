# Psychological Operations

> [!KEY CONCEPT] Cognitive Warfare
> The systemic manipulation of human perception, information flow, and social consensus to steer collective behavior and dismantle critical evaluation.

Psychological Operations are mass or targeted control exercises which weaponize information to manipulate people. There are many of them going on as we speak. They are carried out through TV, social media, billboards, journals, etc.
* Hollywood
* Pornography

# Propaganda

Propaganda is to fabricate a lie and present it as truth, or to radicalize an idea. It is generally used to deify or to demonize an idea during wars. It's also used to exclude groups of people from the general public; it's a mass control weapon.
* Cancel culture
* Black people as gangsters
* Arabs as terrorists
* Religions
