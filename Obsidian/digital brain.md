# The Digital Brain
___
Your **Digital Brain** is an extension of your consciousness built with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/).

Most people treat their computer like a garage, a place to store junk.
We treat it like a **laboratory**, a place to refine thought.

## The Principles
1.  **Local-First**: Every file is stored on your device; there is no need for Wi-Fi or cloud storage.
2.  **Creativity**: You may create canvases, structures, graphs, and drawings with any type of files.
3. **Organization**: Provides organizational depth to your digital assets.
4. **Resonance**: The system should surprise you. It should reveal connections you didn't know existed.

This Garden is the public face of my Digital Brain.
