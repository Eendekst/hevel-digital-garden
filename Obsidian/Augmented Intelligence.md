> [!KEY TAKEAWAY]
> **Augmented Intelligence (True AI)** is the intentional expansion of human cognitive capacity achieved by delegating memory, knowledge structures, and retrieval to digital tools, transforming external Markdown vaults into active extensions of biological intellect. Rather than replacing human thought with black-box automation, Augmented Intelligence elevates human agency through deterministic external substrates.

## What is Augmented Intelligence?

**Augmented Intelligence is the deliberate expansion of human problem-solving and memory capacity through structured external tools.** Instead of trying to hold thousands of uncaptured thoughts, file paths, and project rules in biological working memory, you anchor them into a deterministic, local-first Obsidian vault.

When you record real-world information such as a storage room passcode in a note tagged with concrete YAML metadata (`type: asset`, `category: security`), you convert an ephemeral episodic memory into an objective, queryable database record.

According to [[Hevel/The extended mind theory|The Extended Mind Theory]] (Andy Clark & David Chalmers, 1998), because this note is uniquely yours, customized to your mental schema, and instantly accessible, you automatically endorse its contents without secondary skepticism. The digital tool functionally becomes an extension of your biological mind.

## Raw Artificial Intelligence vs. Augmented Intelligence (True AI)

| Dimension | Raw Artificial Intelligence (Automation) | Augmented Intelligence (Extended Cognition) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cognitive Center** | Algorithmic model / Cloud server | Sovereign human researcher & operator |
| **Trust & Endorsement** | Probabilistic outputs requiring secondary validation | Direct personal knowledge endorsed without skepticism |
| **Memory Substrate** | Transient model weights & ephemeral context windows | Deterministic local files (`.md`, YAML, SQLite) |
| **Primary Objective** | Replacing human task execution (Substitutive) | Expanding biological memory & bandwidth (Augmentative) |
| **Architectural Mode** | Black-box probabilistic inference | Glass-box personal knowledge garden |
| **Failure Mode** | Hallucination, architectural drift, cognitive decay | Verifiable, version-controlled human record |

## How to Build True AI

Augmented Intelligence cannot be attained by simply chatting with a generic cloud AI chatbot. **You must construct its operational environment with folders, Markdown files, YAML schemas, and media assets.**

1. **Externalize Working Memory**: Offload daily ideas, meeting logs, and research into local Markdown notes.
2. **Structure with YAML**: Add frontmatter to every note to create instant machine-readable taxonomy.
3. **Bi-Directionally Link Concepts**: Connect notes using `[[wikilinks]]` to mirror relational associative thinking.
4. **Deploy an Agentic Harness**: Connect local AI tools (like Google Antigravity) to read your vault context, turning the AI into a force multiplier for your own vision.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What does Hevel mean by "True AI"?
**"True AI" is Augmented Intelligence, not artificial substitution.** It is the state where artificial algorithms are anchored to human-curated local context, empowering the individual's sovereign intellect rather than replacing it.

### How does The Extended Mind Theory apply to Obsidian?
**When an external tool is reliable, directly accessible, and transparently trusted, cognitive science treats it as part of the cognitive system itself.** An Obsidian vault functions as an active external cortex—storing knowledge indefinitely and freeing biological brainpower for high-leverage synthesis.
