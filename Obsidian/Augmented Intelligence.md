# Augmented Intelligence is
___
the human way to delegate cognitive functions to external tools (address book, notebooks, media libraries or any other data point) to convert uncaptured or subjective experiences into objective external assets. `` When you record real-world information such as a storage room passcode in your note `Storage passcode`, and tag it with concrete parameters like `asset` and `passcode`, you extract an episodic memory and anchor it into a queryable, semantic database``.

According to the [[The extended mind theory]], because this note is uniquely yours, customized to your thinking, and instantly accessible, you automatically endorse its contents without secondary scepticism, allowing it to behave exactly like an expansion of your biological memory.
