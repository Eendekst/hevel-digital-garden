# Augmented Intelligence

> [!KEY CONCEPT] Augmented Intelligence (Extended Cognition)
> The systemic enhancement of human problem-solving capacity achieved by anchoring episodic memory and unstructured thought into structured, deterministic digital substrates.

Augmented Intelligence is the human way to delegate cognitive functions to external tools (address book, notebooks, media libraries or any other data point) to convert uncaptured or subjective experiences into objective external assets. When you record real-world information such as a storage room passcode in your note `Storage passcode`, and tag it with concrete parameters like `asset` and `passcode`, you extract an episodic memory and anchor it into a queryable, semantic database.

According to the [[The extended mind theory]], because this note is uniquely yours, customized to your thinking, and instantly accessible, you automatically endorse its contents without secondary scepticism, allowing it to behave exactly like an expansion of your biological memory.

### Raw Automation vs. Augmented Intelligence

| Dimension | Raw Artificial Intelligence (Automation) | Augmented Intelligence (Extended Cognition) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cognitive Center** | Algorithmic model / Cloud server | Sovereign human researcher & operator |
| **Trust & Endorsement** | Probabilistic outputs requiring secondary validation | Direct personal knowledge endorsed without skepticism |
| **Memory Substrate** | Transient model weights & ephemeral context windows | Deterministic local files (`.md`, YAML, SQLite) |
| **Primary Objective** | Replacing human task execution | Expanding biological memory & cognitive bandwidth |
| **Architectural Mode** | Black-box probabilistic inference | Glass-box personal knowledge garden |
