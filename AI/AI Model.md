Initially, I wanted to write "if you don't know what an AI model is get out of here" but I'll give you a chance.

An AI model is a system built by an individual or a company on top of what is called a [transformer architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformer_(deep_learning)). This system is the code that renders apps like ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Copilot, etc. Every AI tool comes from an AI model.
