### The truth is 
that I don't know how to read like God reads. But, my notes  give me important cues

The conflict between my analytical mind and faith is [[BRAND/Garden/Atomic Notes/The APPASA Protocol|The APPASA Protocol]]. It's a data analysis procedure I learned on Kaggle.

Apparently, if my data analysis methodology is not rooted in the Bible, my understanding is not unified and complete.

The APPASA protocol will have to be redesigned for more truth.

The goal of the protocol is to approach large scale tasks with a strategic route;

**ASK** - as the first step is not Biblical. We **P**ray first. We pray for guidance because we are not smart enough to know what's best for us.

**PREPARE** - as a second step is wrong if the first step is asking for guidance. The second step naturally has to be to **F**ollow the commands.

**PROCESS** - has to become **A**dapt. Throughout the Bible, every missionary had to face a vast amount of hardships and battles to accomplish their mission. It is important for us to continuously take good, Bible backed decisions. If we're on the right path these decisions should become intuitive.

#### *Observation*
___
Jusqu'à maintenant, je sens que la vraie analyse de donnée est en fait de s'attacher à la matière plutôt que de l'observer;

Pray, Follow, Adapt au lieu de Ask, Prepare, Process.

Par contre, le seul conseil qu'on a par rapport à l'assimilation de données dans ma nouvelle méthode jusqu'à maintenant c'est de s'adapter. C'est flou dans un cadre de science de données.
___

**Analyze** - is a part of adapting (you have to analyze to adapt). However, our God knows every bird in the mountains. If he wants us to spend time analyzing he will make it known. Else, our first step, Prayer should guide us to what we need to do.

**Share** - is definitely the natural next step of Pray, Follow, Adapt, as **s**haring truth is our prophetic duty. Encore une fois, un phénomène qui pousse à l'attachement.
**Act** - ing as the last step is natural, however I believe that the process finishes at the Sharing point.

What can you do more than:

**Praying** for guidance
**Following** what the spirit is convicting you of
**Adapting** to the very difficult path ahead and
**Sharing** the knowledge you gain from it

If you're in Christ?

**Glory be to God.**
